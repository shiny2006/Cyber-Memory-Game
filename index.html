Cybersecurity Glossary

PRIVACY: Safeguarding personal and corporate confidential data from unauthorized exposure.
SECURE: Implementing resilient safeguards and controls to protect systems, data, and users.
BIAS: Systematic favoritism, unfair prejudice, or distortion present in AI algorithms and datasets.
PROMPT: Specific input instructions or queries provided to guide an AI system's generated output.
ALERT: Urgent security notification warning users and administrators of suspicious activity or threats.
DEEPFAKE: Synthetic audio or video media manipulated by AI to impersonate a real individual.
DATA: Digital information assets that must be securely processed, governed, and stored.
AGENT: An autonomous software or AI system designed to perceive its environment and execute tasks.
RISK: The probability and negative business impact of potential cyber vulnerabilities or threats.
PHISHING: Deceptive social engineering attacks designed to trick users into sharing sensitive credentials.
